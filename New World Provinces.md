## Notes

- Every one of the 92 areas has at least one food-producing trade good (grain, fish, cattle, vegetables, fruits, honey, or dates) within it — 11 areas needed a trade-good swap on one territory to satisfy this; the swap always targeted the territory whose terrain best supports that good (farmland/plains → grain, coastal → fish, steppes/mountain → cattle, desert → dates, hills/forest → honey, jungle → fruits).
- Only 12 territories continent-wide keep `province_rank="city"` (up from 96) — one to two per zone, chosen by highest existing `civilization_value`. All other area capitals remain `settlement` rank; they just no longer carry the mechanical city-rank bonus.
- Verification script: `tools/audit_new_world_density.py` (run from the mod root) — confirms area/region counts, full territory coverage with no gaps or duplicates, localization completeness, food coverage, and city-rank count.

## ⚠️ Separate issue found: `map_data/provinces.png` is truncated

While working on this, I found that `map_data/provinces.png` (the province-color bitmap) is missing its PNG end marker — it's an incomplete file, likely from a script that was interrupted mid-write. It fails to load in Pillow and would fail to load in-game or in `tools/generate_minimap.py`. This is unrelated to the area/region restructuring above (which only touched `areas.txt`, `regions.txt`, localization, and the setup file), but it will block the mod from loading until it's restored — e.g. from a backup, or by re-running whatever step last regenerated it. Worth checking before your next playtest.
